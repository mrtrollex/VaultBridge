About

This foundation supports access, explicit space selection and selected status. Browsing notes, Search, Query and Relationships are not available in this Dashboard yet.

Use one canonical HTTPS origin on a dedicated trusted hostname. All ports and proxy routes on that hostname must be trusted; cookies are not isolated by port.

Uncertain cookie mutations require operator-confirmed draining of old issuers, current-key rotation when needed, then clearing cookies and lifecycle records. There is no in-page reset.
